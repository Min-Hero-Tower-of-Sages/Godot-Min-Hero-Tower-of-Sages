# Asset provenance and rights

This is an unofficial Godot rebuild of **Min Hero: Tower of Sages**.
The recovered title artwork identifies Toy Chest Games and SoGood. Their
names are retained for attribution, not to imply endorsement.

Original artwork, audio, the BurbinCasual font, game definitions and
source-derived content were recovered from the reference game supplied for
this project. Original rights remain with their respective rights holders.
Recovery/import is not evidence of permission to redistribute or relicense.

- `content/base/art/source_symbols/`: recovered image exports, with export
  filenames preserved. `symbols.json` records symbol-to-image bindings,
  including shared numeric image aliases.
- Other `content/base/art/`, `content/base/audio/` and `content/base/fonts/`
  files include previously imported recovered media.
- Room payloads and content catalogs derive from the reference game's data
  and source. Provenance fields identify recovery sources; those fields are
  documentation and need not resolve in a normal checkout.

The Godot implementation and conversion/verification tools are maintained in
this project. No blanket license has been selected for the combined repository.
Do not assume third-party media/font files are covered by a license later
selected for original implementation code.

Godot and its export templates have their own license notices. Consult the
engine's license information when distributing a compiled build:
https://godotengine.org/license/

Original extraction trees, recovery caches, local editor/toolchain files,
developer captures/logs and compiled builds are excluded from Git. They have
not been deleted from the developer workspace by repository preparation.
